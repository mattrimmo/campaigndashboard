Campaign revenue

Estimated gross revenue for every record on Listen Up Priorities, from Spotify streams and YouTube Music views. Totals run from release day.
Defaults: Spotify £3.28 per 1,000 (Ditto's figure, £327.75 per 100,000). YouTube Music £1.24 per 1,000 (published official-channel rate of $0.00164 a view at $1 = £0.756). Changes are saved in this browser only.
Spotify revenue = streams × the Spotify rate. YouTube Music revenue = views on the record's "Artist - Topic" upload (the version YouTube Music plays) × the YouTube rate; fan uploads are not counted. Other DSPs are not included: Songstats has no stream counts for Apple Music, Amazon, Deezer or Tidal. Gross figures, before distributor or label cuts. Spotify reports 1 to 2 days late, so the newest days can look flat until the numbers land.
